## Full Description

### Main Headline
**Your Personal Mood Tracker & Emotional Wellness Journal**

Mood Diary helps you understand your emotions better through simple, daily mood tracking. Record how you feel, reflect on your emotional patterns, and nurture your mental wellness—all in a safe, private space.

---

### Why Mood Diary?

**🧠 Understand Yourself Better**
Track your daily moods and discover emotional patterns over time. See what influences your feelings and gain insights into your emotional journey. Build self-awareness, one entry at a time.

**💭 AI-Powered Reflections**
Every mood entry receives a gentle, personalized reflection powered by AI. Get thoughtful perspectives on your emotions that help you process your feelings and find clarity in moments of uncertainty.

**📅 See Your Emotional Timeline**
Beautiful calendar view shows your mood history at a glance. Revisit past entries, notice patterns, and celebrate your emotional growth.

**📊 Visual Insights**
Understand your emotional trends with intuitive charts and statistics. See which emotions dominate your life and track your progress toward better emotional balance.

**🛡️ Your Privacy Matters**
Your emotional data is deeply personal—and we protect it fiercely:
- Military-grade encryption for all data
- Your moods are stored securely in the cloud
- PIN protection and biometric security (Face ID/Touch ID)
- We NEVER share your data with third parties
- Complete data ownership—delete anytime, guilt-free

**🌙 Beautiful Dark Mode**
Easy on the eyes, easy on your device. Perfect for journaling at any time of day.

**🔐 Complete Privacy & Security**
- Secure login with email or Google Sign-In
- PIN code protection
- Face ID / Touch ID biometric unlock
- Your data is encrypted end-to-end
- All biometric data stays on your device only

### How to Use

1. **Open and Create Your Account** - Quick setup with email or Google Sign-In
2. **Log Your Mood** - Tap your emotion and add a note if you'd like
3. **Get a Reflection** - Receive a personalized, thoughtful response
4. **Explore Your Data** - Check your calendar and statistics to see patterns
5. **Nurture Yourself** - Use insights to support your emotional well-being
