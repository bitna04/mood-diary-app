# Privacy Policy - Mood Diary

**Last Updated: May 16, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

Mood Diary ("App," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mood tracking and journaling application.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Account Information
- **Email address**: Used for authentication and account recovery
- **Authentication data**: Managed through Firebase Authentication
- **Google Sign-In credentials**: When you choose to sign in with Google

### 2.2 Mood and Journal Data
- **Mood entries**: Your selected moods and emotional states
- **Journal notes**: Written notes and reflections you add
- **Entry dates and times**: Timestamps associated with your entries

### 2.3 Device Information
- **Biometric data**: Face ID or Touch ID information for app unlock (stored locally on your device)
- **PIN code**: Your security PIN (stored locally on your device, encrypted)
- **Device type and OS**: Basic device information for compatibility

### 2.4 AI Reflection Data
- **Mood context**: We send your mood selection and basic notes to our AI service to generate personalized reflections
- **AI responses**: Generated reflections are stored with your mood entries

## 3. How We Use Your Information

### 3.1 Primary Uses
- **Authentication**: To create and secure your account
- **Service Delivery**: To store, retrieve, and display your mood entries
- **AI Features**: To generate personalized emotional reflections
- **Security**: To protect your account and prevent unauthorized access

### 3.2 Analytics (None)
We do not collect usage analytics or track your behavior. Your emotional data is not used for marketing or profiling.

## 4. Data Storage and Cloud Services

### 4.1 Firebase Services
- **Firebase Authentication**: Securely manages your login credentials
- **Firestore Cloud Database**: Stores your mood entries and journal notes
- **Firebase data centers**: Your data is stored in secure Google data centers

### 4.2 Data Ownership
**You own your data.** All mood entries, notes, and personal information belong to you. We store it as a service provider on your behalf.

## 5. Local Security

### 5.1 Device-Level Protection
- **Biometric authentication**: Face ID/Touch ID verification is processed entirely on your device and never sent to our servers
- **PIN storage**: Your PIN code is encrypted and stored locally on your device only
- **No biometric transmission**: Biometric data never leaves your device

### 5.2 Encryption
- All data transmitted between your device and our servers uses HTTPS encryption
- Sensitive authentication data is encrypted end-to-end

## 6. Data Sharing

### 6.1 We Do NOT Share Your Data
- We do not sell your mood data
- We do not share your emotional information with third parties
- We do not use your data for advertising

### 6.2 Required Sharing
- **Firebase**: Your data is processed through Firebase services (Google's data processor)
- **Google Sign-In**: If you use Google Sign-In, Google receives authentication confirmation only
- **Legal requirements**: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our legal rights

## 7. Third-Party Services

### 7.1 Service Providers
We use the following third-party services:
- **Firebase (Google)**: For authentication, database, and cloud storage
- **Google Sign-In**: For account sign-in options

These services have their own privacy policies, and we recommend reviewing them.

## 8. Data Deletion and Retention

### 8.1 Your Right to Delete
- You can delete your account and all associated data at any time
- Account deletion permanently removes all your mood entries and journal notes
- Deletion cannot be undone

### 8.2 Data Retention
- We retain your data only while your account is active
- Upon account deletion, data is removed from Firestore
- Firebase may retain metadata for a limited period per their data retention policies

## 9. Children's Privacy

Mood Diary is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we become aware of such collection, we will delete the information promptly.

## 10. Your Privacy Rights

### 10.1 Access and Portability
- You have the right to access all your mood entries and personal data
- You can request your data in a portable format

### 10.2 Correction
- You can edit or update your mood entries and personal information at any time

### 10.3 Deletion
- You have the right to delete your account and all associated data
- Simply use the sign-out and account deletion features in the app

## 11. Security Measures

### 11.1 What We Do
- Use Firebase's enterprise-grade security infrastructure
- Encrypt all data in transit with HTTPS
- Implement authentication and authorization controls
- Keep security software and systems updated

### 11.2 Your Responsibility
- Keep your PIN code confidential
- Use a strong, unique password if using email authentication
- Log out of shared devices
- Report any unauthorized access immediately

## 12. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

California residents have the right to:
- Know what personal information we collect
- Delete personal information we've collected
- Opt-out of the sale of personal information (we don't sell data)

To exercise these rights, contact us using the information below.

## 13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or technology. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the updated policy in the app with a new "Last Updated" date.
